The two paid landing pages (/free-reverse-osmosis-system-id/ and /leak-detection-special-tx/) together account for 4,828 views but average only 30–39 seconds on page with a presumed high bounce rate. A/B test: (1) faster load time, (2) phone number above fold, (3) trust signals (reviews, license, guarantee) in first scroll. Each second of improvement can lift CVR 5–10%.

GBP/Maps has the highest engagement rate (24.6%) of all channels. Organic search has a healthy 3.3% CVR. These are the two lowest-cost, highest-quality channels. Publishing 2–3 blog posts/month and optimizing GBP weekly (posts, photos, Q&A) will compound traffic over the next 6–12 months.

Paid Social (Facebook) delivered 256 sessions and just 1 conversion in 90 days — a 0.4% CVR and 5.1% engagement rate, the lowest of any channel. Either pause Facebook Ads entirely and reallocate to Google Ads, or overhaul the audience targeting and creative before next spend cycle.

📋 Social Media Content Guidelines

  • Static posts (2/week): Post Mon/Thu or Tue/Fri. Alternate between educational, promotional, and trust-building content. Mix market-specific and all-market posts.
  • Reels (2/month): Week 1 and Week 3. Keep under 60 seconds. Always include text overlays — 70% of viewers watch without sound. End with a CTA.
  • Review spotlights: At least 1 per month per market. Screenshot the review, add SSP branding. Most engaging content type for service businesses.
  • Market tagging: ID posts → tag "Hayden, Idaho" + #HaydenID #NorthIdaho. TX → tag "Rockwall, TX" + #RockwallTX #DFW. TN → tag "Knoxville, TN" + #KnoxvilleTN.
  • Carousel posts: 5–8 slides max. First slide must stop the scroll. Last slide = always a CTA. Carousels get 3× the reach of single-image posts on Instagram.
  • Phone in every post: Every post should reference the market-specific phone number or direct to "link in bio." Never let a viewer have to search for the number.
  • Response to comments: Reply to all comments within 2 hours during business hours. Social engagement is a Google E-E-A-T signal and a conversion factor.
  • Track CallRail → Social: Create unique tracking numbers per social platform (Facebook, Instagram) to measure which posts drive actual calls.
